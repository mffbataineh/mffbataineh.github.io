Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common technical issues educators encounter with AI tools.

Why isn't ChatGPT/AI tool working in my classroom?

+

Common causes:

  • Network restrictions: Many schools block AI tools at the firewall level. Contact your IT department to request access.
  • Account issues: Ensure you have a valid account and are logged in properly.
  • Service outages: Check the vendor's status page (status.openai.com for ChatGPT).
  • Browser compatibility: Try a different browser or clear your cache/cookies.
  • Rate limits: Free accounts have usage limits. Consider upgrading or waiting.

How do I get my school to unblock AI tools?

+

Strategy:

  1. Document educational purpose: Create a written proposal explaining specific learning objectives
  2. Address concerns: Proactively discuss privacy, academic integrity, and supervision
  3. Propose safeguards: Suggest monitored usage, acceptable use policies, and teacher oversight
  4. Start small: Request pilot program for specific class or project
  5. Provide evidence: Share research and case studies showing educational benefits
  6. Offer training: Propose professional development for proper use

Students can't access the AI tool - what should I do?

+

Troubleshooting steps:

  1. Verify students meet age requirements (13+ for most tools, 18+ for some)
  2. Check if parental consent is required and obtained
  3. Ensure students are using school-approved accounts, not personal ones
  4. Verify school network allows access (test on personal device with mobile data)
  5. Check if tool requires payment/subscription that hasn't been set up
  6. Create a backup plan with alternative tools or offline activities

The AI is giving incorrect or biased information. What do I do?

+

Response protocol:

  • Immediately: Correct the misinformation with students and use as teaching moment
  • Document: Screenshot the problematic output for reporting and future reference
  • Teach critical thinking: Emphasize that AI can be wrong and verification is essential
  • Report to vendor: Most AI companies have feedback mechanisms for quality issues
  • Adjust prompts: Better prompts often yield better results - try rephrasing
  • Set expectations: Make clear that AI is a tool to assist, not replace, learning

How can I detect if students used AI to complete assignments?

+

Detection methods:

  • AI detection tools: Turnitin, GPTZero, Copyleaks (not 100% accurate)
  • Writing style analysis: Compare to previous student work for consistency
  • Oral defense: Ask students to explain their thinking process
  • Process documentation: Require drafts, outlines, and revision history
  • In-class components: Include handwritten or supervised elements

Better approach: Design assignments that make AI less useful (personal reflection, original research, in-class presentation) and teach responsible AI use rather than trying to prevent all use.

The AI tool is too expensive for our budget. Are there free alternatives?

+

Free or low-cost options:

  • ChatGPT Free Tier: Basic but functional for many educational uses
  • Claude.ai Free: Generous free tier with high-quality responses
  • Google Bard/Gemini: Free with Google account
  • Microsoft Copilot: Free with Microsoft account
  • Khan Academy: Completely free adaptive learning platform
  • Grammarly Free: Basic writing assistance
  • Google Translate: Free translation tool

Grant opportunities: Many vendors offer education discounts or grants. Check with DonorsChoose, local education foundations, or apply directly to companies.

Data privacy concerns - is it safe to use AI tools with student data?

+

Safety checklist:

  • Use only FERPA/COPPA/GDPR-compliant tools
  • Review vendor privacy policies carefully
  • Never input personally identifiable information (PII) into free AI tools
  • Use school-managed accounts, not personal consumer accounts
  • Get proper consent from parents/guardians
  • Ensure data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Verify vendor doesn't use student data for AI training without consent
  • Check that vendor will delete data upon request

See our Ethics & Privacy Guidelines for comprehensive guidance.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Guides

ChatGPT Issues

ChatGPT Not Loading or Responding

1 Check Service Status: Visit status.openai.com to see if there's an outage
2 Clear Browser Cache: Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then restart the browser
3 Try Different Browser: Test in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge to rule out browser-specific issues
4 Check Network: Ensure you're connected to internet and ChatGPT isn't blocked by firewall
5 Log Out and Back In: Sign out completely, close browser, reopen, and sign back in
6 Check Account Status: Verify your account is active and subscription is current (if applicable)

Google Workspace / Classroom Integration

AI Extensions Not Working in Google Workspace

1 Verify Admin Approval: School Google Workspace admin must approve third-party apps
2 Check Permissions: Ensure the extension has necessary permissions (may require re-authorization)
3 Update Extension: Remove and reinstall the extension to get latest version
4 Review Organizational Policies: Some school policies block certain types of add-ons

LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle) Integration

AI Tool Won't Connect to Learning Management System

1 Verify LTI Settings: Check that LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability) is enabled in LMS
2 Update API Keys: Ensure API keys/credentials are current and correctly entered
3 Check User Roles: Verify you have instructor/admin privileges to add external tools
4 Contact LMS Admin: Integration may require institutional-level setup
5 Review Vendor Documentation: Each LMS has specific integration requirements - consult both LMS and AI tool documentation
