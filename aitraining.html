Our comprehensive training programs are designed to equip educators with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to integrate AI effectively in their classrooms. Whether you're just starting out or looking to become an AI champion in your institution, we have a program tailored to your needs.

  • Practical, Hands-On Learning: Real classroom applications, not just theory

  • Ongoing Support: Access to community forums, office hours, and resources
  • Certification: Earn recognized credentials to showcase your expertise

Specialized Workshops & Mini-Courses

Short, focused sessions on specific topics. Perfect for targeted professional development.

Technical Skills Workshops (2-4 hours each)

  • Prompt Engineering for Educators: Master the art of getting the best results from AI language models
  • AI Image Generation for Education: Create custom visuals with DALL-E, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion
  • Building Custom GPTs: Design specialized AI assistants for your classroom
  • AI Voice and Video Tools: Leverage text-to-speech, dubbing, and AI video creation

Subject-Specific Mini-Courses (4-6 hours each)

  • AI in STEM Education: Applications for math, science, and engineering classrooms
  • AI for Language Learning: Translation, conversation practice, and language acquisition
  • AI in Social Studies: Primary source analysis, debate facilitation, and research support
  • AI for Arts & Humanities: Creative writing, music generation, and art analysis

Policy & Ethics Seminars (3-4 hours each)

  • Student Data Privacy & FERPA Compliance: Legal requirements and best practices
  • AI Bias & Equity: Recognizing and mitigating algorithmic bias in education
  • Academic Integrity in the AI Age: Honor codes, detection, and teaching responsible use
  • Accessibility & Universal Design: Ensuring AI benefits all learners

Certification Programs

Earn recognized credentials that demonstrate your AI expertise to students, parents, and administrators.

CERTIFICATE

Certified AI Educator (CAE)

⏱️ 3-6 months
📊 Portfolio-based

Demonstrate comprehensive AI literacy and effective classroom implementation through a rigorous portfolio assessment.

  • Complete 40 hours of core training
  • Implement AI tools in classroom for minimum 3 months
  • Submit portfolio of lesson plans and student work
  • Pass written assessment on AI fundamentals and ethics
  • Present case study to panel of educators

AI Integration Specialist

Corporate Training Programs
