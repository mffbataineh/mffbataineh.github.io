📋 About This Analysis

This report represents a preliminary analysis of VR integration opportunities based on review of existing course syllabi. It comprehensively covers technical content (laboratory experiments and theoretical visualization needs), and provides initial suggestions for field trips and professional development.

⚠️ Faculty Input Needed: Sections on soft skills training, co-curricular activities, and specific professional development needs require input from course instructors and coordination with the Engineering Students Support Hub to ensure alignment with student needs and departmental objectives.

This document is intended as a starting point for faculty discussion and refinement before final submission to the VR Content Development Task Force.

📊 All VR Integration Opportunities - Five Categories

Categories Covered:

  • ✅ Laboratory Experiments & Practical Sessions (10 lab courses)
  • ✅ Complex Theoretical Concepts Requiring Visualization (11 theory courses)
  • ✅ Field Trips & Site Visits (3 virtual field trip opportunities)
  • ⚠️ Soft Skills Training & Simulations (initial suggestions; faculty input needed)
  • ⚠️ Co-Curricular Activities (requires input from Engineering Students Support Hub)

🔴 HIGH PRIORITY - Complex Visualization Needs

ECOM 412 Electromagnetic Waves Theory
▼

🎯 VR Content Focus

3D electromagnetic field visualization

📚 Specific Topics

  • Time-varying electromagnetic fields and Maxwell's equations
  • Plane wave propagation in different media (dielectric, lossy, conducting)
  • Electric and magnetic field distributions
  • Transmission line wave propagation
  • Standing waves and reflection phenomena
  • Waveguide modes (TE and TM)
Learning Benefit: Students struggle to visualize invisible EM fields; VR can show field patterns, propagation, and how fields interact with materials in 3D space
✓ Faculty Input: Dr. Oguzhan Akgol has already identified this as a priority need
ELEC 325 Engineering Electromagnetics Theory
▼

🎯 VR Content Focus

Electric and magnetic field visualization

📚 Specific Topics

  • Electric field lines and equipotential surfaces
  • Magnetic flux and field lines (Gauss's law, Ampere's law)
  • Boundary conditions at material interfaces
  • Faraday's law and induced fields
  • Transmission line theory
Learning Benefit: Foundation course where students first encounter field concepts; VR visualization critical for building intuition
ELEC 481 Electric Energy Conversion Lab Lab
▼

🎯 VR Content Focus

Virtual electrical machines laboratory

📚 Specific Lab Experiments

  • Transformer operation (magnetic flux, core saturation, equivalent circuits)
  • DC motor/generator operation and characteristics
  • 3-phase synchronous generator under different loads
  • Induction motor torque-speed characteristics
  • Rotating magnetic fields visualization
Learning Benefit: Safe environment to visualize dangerous high-voltage equipment, magnetic field rotation, and machine internals
⚠️ Safety Advantage: Students can practice procedures before handling actual high-voltage equipment
